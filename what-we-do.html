OUR APPROACH

Meeting mob where
they're at.

There isn't one pathway to wellbeing. SAMS is working towards a practical, non-judgemental model informed by Aboriginal community values and lived experience.

Peer connection

Peer mentoring is being explored as a way for people with lived experience to share encouragement and connection. Future workers will need clearly defined roles, training, supervision and referral boundaries.

Safe places to yarn

We're planning community yarning spaces that welcome people across different stages of their journey. Participation will not be described as treatment or crisis care.

Navigation and referral

We want to help people find the right qualified services for needs such as housing, treatment, wellbeing and practical support, once referral pathways have been established.

Availability: These program areas are proposals and are not yet advertised as active services. SAMS does not currently operate a crisis hotline on this website.
