Our story

WHO WE ARE

Built with community.
Grounded in understanding.

SAMS stands for Substance Affected Mob Support. We are an Aboriginal-led initiative developing practical and culturally respectful pathways for mob affected by alcohol and other drugs.

We believe lived experience has value. People deserve to be listened to and supported without being reduced to their circumstances. Our aim is to foster connection and help community members make informed choices in their own time.

Our principles

  • Respect for every person and their choices
  • Cultural safety, belonging and connection
  • Peer understanding and community involvement
  • Privacy, clear boundaries and responsible referrals
  • Listening before assuming what someone needs

SAMS is in its establishment stage. Please check the Programs page for confirmed availability as our work develops.
