SAMS · Privacy and confidentiality

Your privacy matters

PILOT PRIVACY NOTICE · 20 OCTOBER 2026

Respect, dignity
and privacy.

Substance Affected Mob Support (SAMS) is an Aboriginal-led peer-support and service-navigation initiative. We support community connection and help people find existing services; we do not replace clinical treatment or emergency services.

What our website currently collects

The public website includes general-enquiry, volunteer and partnership forms. These send your selected contact details to FormSubmit, a third-party form-forwarding provider, which forwards them to the SAMS administration inbox after email activation. FormSubmit also stores submitted data for a limited period. Please include only general administrative questions and avoid health details, alcohol or drug histories, family information or other sensitive material.

Our approved team members can create staff accounts and manage non-sensitive website content, program status, administrative tasks and organisational contacts. This pilot may store account identifiers, roles, MFA authentication records, task assignments and non-sensitive partnership details in our database.

What we do not collect through this pilot

We do not currently accept online client intake, confidential case notes, personal health records or sensitive peer-support narratives. Client and form-submission database tables have been kept inaccessible through the public application. Please do not put such details into general task descriptions, partnership notes or contact emails.

How information is used

Staff account details and non-sensitive operational data are used only to operate SAMS's website, manage approved organisational work, and control access to the staff area. Staff access depends on their approved role and multifactor verification.

Where data is processed

The website is hosted on Render, and the pilot database uses Supabase's Sydney region. Third-party networks and authentication services may process technical information across other locations. Render, Supabase and email providers may process metadata needed to deliver or secure their services. The free pilot does not provide a production-grade backup or disaster-recovery guarantee.

Security and retention

SAMS uses separate staff accounts, authenticator verification and database role-based permissions for its pilot. Routine operational records should be kept only as necessary and removed when no longer needed, subject to any applicable legal requirements. SAMS is still finalising its formal retention, incident-response and access procedures before enabling confidential case management.

Your rights and privacy concerns

To ask about an account, request correction or deletion of non-sensitive information, or raise a privacy concern, contact sam.support.admin@gmail.com. Do not include documents or sensitive personal histories in the initial email. SAMS will need to verify identity before responding to requests for account records.

Confidentiality boundary: SAMS is not operating an online case-intake service through this website. If you need immediate help, call 000. You can also contact 13YARN on 13 92 76 or Victorian DirectLine on 1800 888 236.

This notice describes the current website pilot, not a legal certification or a promise that all proposed future SAMS services are operational. A formal privacy policy and independent review are required before confidential records can be activated.
