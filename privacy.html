This is a website preview. Public enquiry forms, staff login and CRM access are not enabled on this version.

Do not submit private client information, health details, alcohol or drug histories, identification documents or other sensitive information through unverified channels.

The future SAMS service will need a detailed privacy policy covering what data is collected, lawful use and disclosure, access controls, retention, complaint handling, security and contacts. That policy must be reviewed and approved before confidential intake becomes operational.

As with most public websites, the hosting provider may process routine technical information such as IP addresses and access logs.

General administrative enquiries can be sent to sam.support.admin@gmail.com without including sensitive personal information.
