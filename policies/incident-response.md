# SAMS security and privacy incident response
**Working procedure · 20 October 2026 · Requires organisational adoption**

## Trigger events
Examples: stolen devices, lost credentials, suspicious sign-ins, unauthorised account access, misdirected email, compromised website, unexpected exposure of private records, or accidental collection of confidential participant information.

## Immediate steps
1. Ensure anyone facing immediate physical danger has access to emergency assistance (000).
2. Contain the incident: disable affected accounts or sessions, rotate secrets, block relevant tokens or close affected systems while preserving necessary evidence.
3. Notify the nominated SAMS privacy/security lead promptly; limit discussion to those who need to know.
4. Record the time, affected system, initial facts and containment steps without distributing private data through normal email or chat.

## Assessment
Identify records and people possibly affected, sensitivity, timeframe, recipients, likelihood of misuse and any harm-reducing measures. Determine applicable Victorian and Commonwealth reporting duties, including the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme where relevant. Assess suspected eligible breaches promptly; seek independent legal/security help if needed. A serious eligible breach may require notification to affected people and the OAIC as soon as practicable.

## Recovery
Restore from known-good versions after forensic and access checks. Confirm patches and permissions, and review logs. Monitor for recurrence. Do not reopen confidential intake until the incident is contained and safeguards have been re-tested.

## Follow-up
Document decisions, notifications, root causes, policy amendments and training. Communicate respectfully, support affected people and ensure a committee-level review.
