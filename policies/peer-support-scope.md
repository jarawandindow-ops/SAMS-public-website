# SAMS peer-support scope and safeguarding procedure
**Working procedure · 20 October 2026 · For committee review before confidential service delivery**

## Purpose
Substance Affected Mob Support (SAMS) is a community-led, Aboriginal-led peer-support and service-navigation initiative. Its role is to support social connection, wellbeing, cultural safety and practical access to existing services. SAMS does not replace medical practitioners, AOD clinicians, professional counselling, emergency response, statutory child protection, housing providers or accredited treatment services.

## What peer workers can offer
- Respectful yarning, listening and non-judgemental emotional support, within training and supervision boundaries.
- Assistance identifying immediate practical goals, finding contact details and understanding available community services.
- Supporting *voluntary*, informed referrals to qualified providers.
- Facilitating planned group activities under documented safety rules and supervisory arrangements.
- Explaining clearly when a matter is outside SAMS's remit.

## What peer workers cannot promise
- Clinical diagnoses, treatment plans, medical advice, detoxification or prescribing.
- Guaranteed confidentiality in circumstances where immediate risk, safeguarding or lawful disclosure obligations require a response.
- Immediate crisis intervention or 24/7 availability.
- Guarantees of housing, court or child-protection outcomes.
- Recording participants' personal histories in general organisational tasks or emails.

## Immediate risk and escalation
1. If someone is in immediate danger, call 000.
2. For Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people in crisis, 13YARN is available on 13 92 76.
3. For Victorian alcohol and other drug information/referrals, use DirectLine 1800 888 236.
4. Inform the designated SAMS supervisor as soon as safely practicable, share only what is necessary, and follow applicable safeguarding and mandatory reporting obligations.
5. No worker should attempt to provide a substitute for clinical assessment or emergency response.

## Before a formal peer service opens
Committee to approve role descriptions, volunteer screening, induction, supervision, referral pathways, conflict-of-interest procedure, incident response, privacy training, and escalation contacts. Participation should be voluntary and non-punitive, with informed consent and the ability to withdraw. Confirm culturally safe practices with relevant communities.
