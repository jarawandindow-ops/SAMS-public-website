# SAMS privacy, records and retention procedure
**Draft for organisational adoption · 20 October 2026**

## Scope
Covers SAMS's website, staff accounts, operational records, volunteering and future referral activities. As a peer-support organisation, SAMS may still have obligations under the Victorian Health Records Act 2001 if it handles health information. The Australian Privacy Act 1988 may also apply depending on services and activities. SAMS must not assume it is exempt simply because it is volunteer-led or small.

## Collection limitation
Collect only information necessary for the documented purpose; explain why and how it will be used. Allow anonymous or pseudonymous access to general information wherever lawful and practicable. The present website pilot must not collect private case records, alcohol/drug histories, medical diagnoses, children’s details or confidential peer-support narratives.

## Account information
For operational access, store only the authorised person's account ID, display name, email within authentication provider, approved role, MFA factor and organisational access history. Obtain explicit agreement to staff account terms. Never use shared passwords.

## Staff access
Each staff member has an individual account and strong password with Microsoft Authenticator compatible TOTP. Owner and administrator permissions do not imply a right to read future confidential client records. Restrict access to the minimum role necessary; suspend departing staff promptly. A record of access changes is kept in a private audit log.

## Information use and disclosure
Use personal information for the purpose communicated on collection. Obtain consent where required for referrals and disclosures. Disclose the minimum necessary, and avoid putting identifiable personal health material into email or ordinary tasks. Account for lawful exceptions and safeguarding responsibilities.

## Accuracy, access and correction
Provide a clear path for an individual to request access or correction. Verify identity privately before releasing data. Provide a documented procedure for reviewing, acting on and recording a request. Initial general enquiries: sam.support.admin@gmail.com, without sensitive attachments.

## Retention and deletion
Pilot website drafts, ordinary staff tasks and partner contacts are organisational records, not client records. The committee should adopt a retention schedule that accounts for applicable legal retention duties and potential complaints. Do not invent a universal deletion period for all records; identify record category, legal basis, retention owner, review frequency and secure disposal method. Disable unnecessary exports, and remove operational data that is no longer required within lawful limits.

## Security
Use MFA, HTTPS, least-privilege database policies, private audit events, patching, device locks, backup recovery tests and emergency revocation. Free-tier hosting must not be treated as a sufficient backup plan for sensitive client records. Avoid personal Gmail delivery of health narratives.

## Public transparency
Publish a plain-language privacy collection notice before any public form accepts data, identify the legal operator, disclose relevant processors where possible, and explain access/correction/complaints options. Obtain independent Australian legal and technical review before confidential case intake is activated.
